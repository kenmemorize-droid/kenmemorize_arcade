Kopi Rush

Read the order, pour it right, swipe it out. 60 seconds behind the kopitiam counter.
How to Play

An order comes in, like Kopi O Siew Dai. Tap the stations to pour each ingredient the right number of times.

A wrong tap — something that isn't in the drink, or one pour too many — stuns you for a second. Nothing wrong ever lands in the cup.

When the drink is complete, swipe the cup up to serve it. Serve as many as you can before the clock runs out.

Full Rules

How a rush works, and how to read any order.
  1. Each order shows its kopitiam name, in English and Chinese. In Display mode the ticket also lists every ingredient and how many pours it needs. In Recall mode you only get the name.
  2. Tap a station once per pour. The order you pour in doesn't matter.
  3. Tapping something the drink doesn't use, or pouring one too many, is a wrong tap: the counter locks for 1 second and nothing goes in the cup.
  4. When every ingredient is in, the cup glows. Swipe it up (or press ↑ / Space) to serve. An unfinished cup won't go.
  5. Every pour in a served drink is worth 10 points, and a drink made with no wrong taps earns +20 more. The rush lasts 60 seconds.
Kopi lingo — the recipe rules
Kopi咖啡2 coffee + 2 hot water, sweetened with 2 condensed milk.
Teh茶Same as Kopi, with tea in place of coffee.
Cham鸳鸯 Yuan YangCoffee and tea mixed: 1 coffee + 1 tea + 2 hot water, 2 condensed milk.
O乌No milk. Sweetened with 2 sugar instead.
C西1 evaporated milk, plus 2 sugar.
Siew Dai少甜Less sweet: the sweetener (sugar, or condensed milk) drops to 1.
Ga Dai加甜More sweet: the sweetener goes up to 3.
Kosong无糖No sugar at all. Only for O and C — condensed milk is already sweet.
Gao厚Strong: 3 coffee/tea, only 1 hot water.
Po薄Weak: 1 coffee/tea, 3 hot water.
Di Lo滴露Extra thick: 3 coffee/tea and no hot water.
Peng冰Iced: add 1 ice.
Kopi C Gao Siew Dai Peng → Gao: 3 coffee, 1 hot water · C: 1 evaporated milk · Siew Dai: 1 sugar · Peng: 1 ice.
