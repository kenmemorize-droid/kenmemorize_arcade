MemoMath

Memorise each coloured digit. Then recall it and do the maths
How to Play

Coloured tiles flash up, each holding one digit. Memorise them before the bar runs out.

The digits hide and every tile shows a rule instead, like − 3. Apply it to the digit that was there and keep only the last digit of the answer.

Tap a tile, key in its result, fill them all, then press Enter.

Full Rules

The complete round, step by step.
  1. Each digit flashes inside its own coloured tile. Memorise them before the timer runs out.
  2. The digits then hide. Each tile now shows only an operation, like − 3.
  3. Recall the digit that was in that tile, apply its operation, and keep only the last digit of the result.
  4. Tap a tile and key in its result. Fill every tile, then submit.
