Memory Mash

Study the board, then recall what was on it
How to Play

A board of items appears. Memorise as many of them as you can before it goes.

In How Many? the whole board hides, and you tap every item you saw from a mixed bank of options.

In What's Missing? a few items vanish from the board — tap each gap and pick what used to be there.

One point per correct item. Competition mode adds fixed timers and gives you a single attempt.
