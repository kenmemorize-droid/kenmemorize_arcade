RingMatch

Slide the balls until every one sits inside a ring of its own colour

How to Play

Swipe a row sideways, or a column up and down, to slide the balls one cell. The coloured rings never move — only the balls do.

A ball pushed off an edge wraps around to the other side of the board.

Land every ball inside a ring of its own colour to clear the board.
