VOXEL VAULT

Four spatial puzzles, each with the answer taken away before you get to answer it

How to play
  1. Pick a mode, a difficulty and how long you get to study.
  2. A structure appears. Look at it hard — the timer is the whole point.
  3. The structure disappears. There is no going back to it.
  4. Answer from memory. With 2–4 players everyone studies together, then answers in private turns.
  5. 10 points a round. Highest total after the last round takes it.
