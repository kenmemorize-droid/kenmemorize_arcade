Cart Recall

Read the list · Clear the aisle

How to play

  1. A shopping list flashes on screen. Memorize it.
  2. The list vanishes, then the shelves appear.
  3. Tap every item that was on your list.
  4. Shelves are stocked with lookalikes — pear beside apple, bagel beside pretzel.
  5. Three wrong grabs ends your run. The glance gets shorter every round.
