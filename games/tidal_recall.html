TIDAL RECALL

Every wave scatters the shore. Find the treasure you haven't seen before

How to Play

The tide drops two treasures on the grid. Tap either one to begin.

The shore washes blank and everything reappears in new positions — plus one treasure you have never seen. Tap that new one. Position tells you nothing, so you have to remember the treasures themselves.

Tap something you have already seen and it costs a life; the one you missed is revealed in green.

The grid and the pile grow every wave, later beaches add mirrored and re-tinted lookalikes, and surviving a full beach banks a bonus of finds × 100.

Mode

Three beaches on a growing grid. Run out of lives and you wash on to the next.

Settings

Shared: one shore keeps growing between you, so you must remember what your opponents saw too.
