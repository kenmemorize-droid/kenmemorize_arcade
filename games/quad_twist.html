Quad Twist

Rotate the tiles to rebuild the target pattern
How to Play
  1. Tap a rotate knob on the board to spin the four tiles around it one step clockwise.
  2. Keep twisting until the board matches the target picture beside the clock.
  3. Classic matches colours only. Numbers makes every digit line up too.
  4. The clock starts on your first twist. Reset replays the same puzzle.
