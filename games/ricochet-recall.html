Summary

Full rules

  1. Study the grid. Each yellow diagonal is a mirror — memorise where they sit.
  2. The mirrors vanish and a green arrow appears on the edge. That's where the ball enters and which way it's heading.
  3. The ball travels in a straight line until it hits a mirror, then turns 90°. A / sends a rightward ball up; a \ sends it down.
  4. Tap the edge circle where you think the ball comes out, then confirm.
  5. Every three stages the grid grows and gains mirrors. Solo costs a life on a miss and replays the stage; pass & play scores a point per player, per round.

The ball can never leave through the arrow it came in by, so that circle is never a valid answer. In pass & play everyone studies the same board together, then answers in private with a 30-second turn clock.
