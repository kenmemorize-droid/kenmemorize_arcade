Star Slide

Your move Tap one of your counters, or swipe it in a direction.

    How to play

    • Each side starts with five counters on its home row. The middle counter carries the star.
    • Red moves first. On your turn, slide one of your own counters up, down, left or right.
    • A counter keeps sliding until it hits the edge of the board or another counter. It can't stop early or jump over anything.
    • Stop your star on the centre square, c3, to win. Any counter can stop there, so the centre can be blocked too.

    A star only stops when something is in its way. Park a counter right next to the centre, then slide your star in from the opposite side.
