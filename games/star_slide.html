Star Slide

Slide your counters, set up a blocker, and land your star on the centre first.
How to Play

Each side starts with five counters on its home row. The one in the middle carries the star.

On your turn, slide one of your counters up, down, left or right. It keeps going until it hits the edge or another counter.

First to stop their star on the centre square wins. Every other counter is a blocker, for you or against you.

Full Rules

Everything a game of Star Slide can throw at you.
  1. Red's five counters start along the bottom row and Green's along the top. The middle counter of each row carries the star.
  2. Red moves first, then turns alternate. On your turn, slide one of your own counters up, down, left or right.
  3. A sliding counter keeps going until it hits the edge of the board or another counter. It can't stop early, jump over anything or capture.
  4. Win by stopping your star on the centre square, c3. Sliding over the centre doesn't count. Any counter may stop on the centre, so it can be blocked.
  5. If you have no legal slide, your turn is skipped. There is no draw rule: if nobody can break through, start a new game.

Strategy
A star only stops when something is in its way. Park a counter right next to the centre, then slide your star in from the opposite side. Watch the other star too: one blocker in the right place stops it cold.
