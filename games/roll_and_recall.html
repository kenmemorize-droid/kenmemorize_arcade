📖 How to Play

  1. Each face is mapped to an orange die (row) + blue die (column)
  2. Memorize as many faces as you can before time runs out
  3. In the Recall round, two dice are rolled — pick the matching face!
  4. Players take turns in order (1 → 2 → 3 … → back to 1) each round
  5. First player to reach 5 points wins 🏆
