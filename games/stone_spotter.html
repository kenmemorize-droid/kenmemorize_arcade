Stone Spotter

Memorize the weiqi board — then spot the stones that sneaked in

How to Play

A weiqi (Go) board appears with black and white stones. Memorize where they sit before the timer runs out.

Then the board returns with a few new stones mixed in. Tap the stones you think are new — the counter at the top tells you how many to find.

Every wrong tap costs you accuracy, so look carefully. Stuck? Give up to reveal the answer.
