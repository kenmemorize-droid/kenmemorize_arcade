Hue Recall

Rebuild the missing brand colour from memory

How to play

A brand logo appears with one colour greyed out. Drag the H, S and B sliders to mix the shade you remember — the logo repaints as you drag.

Tap the tick to lock it in. The real brand colour is revealed and you score 0–10 on how close you got.

Solo plays a set number of logos, Endless runs until you miss, Practice is unscored, and Pass & Play splits the tile between two players.

Full rules
  1. A brand logo appears with one colour greyed out. On a single-colour mark that's the whole logo; on a multi-colour one the rest stays true, so only the missing part is yours to recall.
  2. Drag the H, S and B sliders to mix the colour you remember. The logo repaints live as you drag.
  3. Tap ✓ to lock your answer. The true brand colour is revealed.
  4. You're scored 0–10 on how close you got, measured in CIE Lab so it matches how the eye sees difference.
  5. Endless runs until a round drops below your threshold. Practice is untimed with no score.
  6. Pass & Play puts both players on one screen — the tile splits so each side shows that player's mix.
  7. Which colour gets asked is randomised — Pepsi might want the red one round and the blue the next.
  8. Share the challenge code to give someone the exact same logo set.
