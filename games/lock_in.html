How to Play

Each room has objects on pedestals. Study everything — how many leaves, candles or fish, and what colour things are.

Walk the hall to the brick wall. It asks one question. Set your answer with − / +, then hit LOCK IN.

The question isn't always about the room you just left. It can ask about the previous room, or up to three rooms back — the wall always says which.

One wrong answer and the floor drops. Rooms get busier the deeper you go.
