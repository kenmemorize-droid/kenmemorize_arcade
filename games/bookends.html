How to Play

Every round deals two letters: a first and a last.

Race to type a real word that starts with the first and ends with the last, at least 3 letters long. P … W? Pillow.

The first valid word wins the round. A word that doesn't count just bounces, so keep trying. If nobody gets one in 30 seconds, nobody scores, and you'll see a few answers that would have worked.

First to 7 rounds wins the match.
