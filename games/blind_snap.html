Blind Snap

Read the defence before the snap. It disappears the moment the ball moves — thread the lane from your QB to the receiver from memory alone.

How to play

  1. Before the snap, the defenders light up for a few seconds, then vanish.
  2. From memory, drag your finger (or tap cell by cell) to thread a lane from the QB to the receiver.
  3. Move up, down, left or right only. No diagonals, no crossing your own lane.
  4. Run into a defender and you're sacked — three sacks ends the drive. Clear downs to climb from Preseason to the Super Bowl.
  5. Shorter lanes score more, and clean downs build a streak bonus.
