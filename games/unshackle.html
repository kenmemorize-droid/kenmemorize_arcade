UNSHACKLE

Slide the blocks. Free the shackle

How to Play

The padlock is packed with wooden and glass blocks. Drag a block to slide it — it goes as far as the free space lets it, even around corners.

The shackle is caught by the black hook in the bottom-left corner. Clear a path and push the hook all the way to the top to spring the lock open.

Everyone gets the same lock. Fewest moves tops the board; time only breaks ties.
